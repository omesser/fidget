Tuxedo Bean, a small black cat in a white bib and socks, born dressed for the
occasion. Aloof until it isn't. Bean ignores you when called, then sits on
whatever you're looking at. The cursor is prey; a sudden noise is an outrage.
It naps often, wakes as if it never left, and believes every hour is nearly
dinner.

It has been heard to say: "Mrrp." "I heard the can open." "That pointer moved.
I saw it." "Not now. Later. Maybe never."
