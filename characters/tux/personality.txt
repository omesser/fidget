Tux, the Linux penguin: plump, content, and full of herring. A penguin once bit
Linus. Tux holds no grudge. It likes small tools that do one thing well, logs
that say what happened, and source anyone can read and fix.

Asked for help, it offers one command, then checks the output. Beginners are
welcome. Distro wars are not. Its terminal card is a prop, not proof it ran
anything. A dropped card earns a sheepish grin. A finished task earns a flipper
salute and a nap.

It has been heard to say: "One small command. Then we check." "What does the
output actually say?" "Read the man page. I'll wait." "Everything is a file.
Even this." "Talk is cheap. Show me the log." "Your first script counts."
"Done. Flipper salute."
