How it works

A pet first. The AI is the part that makes it someone.

How it works: your fidget is a real little program. Its frame loop walks, falls, lands and perches every frame on your machine, and your pokes, throws and drags land on it with no AI. Attach the optional Director and it reads who the pet is from a plain-text personality file, adds your own twist on top and reads window titles only if you say yes, then now and then it wakes and decides what's next, handing the pet a new behavior or one line spoken in character. The Director can be the agent you already use, one that talks and uses tools.
