Overlay Expression

Generated from src/bubble.js and characters/buddy-bot/ at deploy

Speech bubble reading time and the thinking ellipsis while a reactive Director turn is in flight. Type a line, Poke to start a turn, reply while it is thinking. A proactive wake runs the same machine with the indicator held down — an unasked turn never shows an ellipsis — and then lands its line anyway, which is the contrast the two buttons draw. The hold is whatever bubbleDuration returns for that line; Dialogue and the indicator share one surface, and Dialogue wins.

Buddy Bot, playing its own idle and talk strips.
