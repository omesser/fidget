Fidget design pages

Pages that have to run to be judged. Everything else about the project lives in the repository, which renders it better than this can.

  • Character Gallery
    Every Character this project can publish, and all nine Required Animations, playing at the fps its own Character Manifest declares. The one thing a repository cannot show you: a walk cycle in motion.
    Generated from characters/ at deploy
  • Fidget Cues
    The visual and audio cue for each interaction — click, double-click, right-click, pickup, drop. Hover the sprite for 1.5s and the quick-message pill appears, focused, above it. Play each cue, or try the gestures on a draggable BMO.
    Dated proposal, #277
  • Fidget Bubble
    The speech bubble and thinking indicator the overlay draws, in the chat surface's type and shape but not its fills — over a light, a dark and a busy desktop, beside the design it retired. The quick-message pill reuses that chrome with no tail: empty, "talk to me", accent Send disc. Frozen, it says "Nothing can answer yet" and Send is a dim disc. The thing a stylesheet cannot show you: whether a border survives a photograph.
    Dated design, #441 — shipped by #456
  • Fidget Chat
    The chat surface a Summon opens: a turn in flight while the character keeps moving, an answer arriving as Speech and a Behavior, and the state with no Harness attached. Drive all three.
    Dated proposal, #17
  • Chat Surface, Three Ways
    One conversation in the chat surface #17 opens, drawn three ways — modern minimal, terminal log, pane of glass — so a direction can be picked. Drive all three; a switch keeps the turn that is already streaming.
    Dated proposal, #339
  • Overlay Expression
    Speech bubble reading time and the thinking ellipsis while a reactive Director turn is in flight, over Buddy Bot's own frames. Type a line, Poke, reply, or take an unasked wake and watch the ellipsis never arrive — github.com cannot hold a bubble for you.
    Generated from src/bubble.js and characters/buddy-bot/ at deploy
  • Window Names Hint
    Three ways to tell a user their character knows where the windows are but not what they are — a chat notice, a speech bubble, a menu-bar badge — on a mock desktop you can arm from each trigger the issue names. Dismiss one and relaunch: the page proves the dismissal survives.
    Dated proposal, #916
